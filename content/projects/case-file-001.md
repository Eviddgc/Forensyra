## Objective

Build one strong, complete investigation from public sample evidence and publish the full report openly. Every analyst works the part of the case that matches their role, and the Lead Forensic Investigator combines the findings into a single, coherent report.

## How it works

- **Evidence:** Public, legal sample images — a disk image, a memory dump, a packet capture, and supporting logs.
- **Roles:** Endpoint, network, memory and malware, timeline and reporting, evidence custody, and legal review all contribute.
- **Output:** One published investigation report on GitHub, plus supporting write-ups from each analyst.

## Scope and safety

This case uses only legal practice material and public sample evidence. No real victim data or unauthorised investigation is involved.

## Deliverables

- A full investigation report (published on GitHub)
- Per-role analysis notes and artefacts
- A reconstructed incident timeline
- A short summary write-up suitable for a portfolio
