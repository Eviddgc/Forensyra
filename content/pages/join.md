We are welcoming people interested in every part of digital forensics — from evidence handling and endpoint analysis to network, memory, mobile, cloud, and AI forensics.

## What you can develop

- Practical digital-forensics experience
- Evidence-handling and documentation skills
- Analytical and investigative thinking
- Technical communication and reporting
- Collaboration and project-management experience
- A stronger evidence-led portfolio

## How selection works

- Applications are collected through our short online form (about 3 minutes).
- **Limited spots.** Selected members will be contacted by email.
- Beginners with genuine interest are welcome — you only need the required questions.

All activities focus on **legal practice cases, public sample evidence, privacy, consent, and responsible learning.** No real victim data or unauthorised investigations are ever used.
