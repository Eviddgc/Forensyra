description: "How Forensyra works legally, ethically, and responsibly with digital evidence."
---

These rules apply to every member and every activity. They are not optional.

## The core commitments

1. **Legal practice cases only.** We work exclusively on authorised, educational material.
2. **Public sample evidence only.** Disk images, memory dumps, and packet captures come from public, legal sources.
3. **No real victim data.** We never use or request real case data, personal data, or leaked material.
4. **Privacy and consent.** We respect privacy and obtain consent where it applies.
5. **Responsible learning.** Our aim is defence, investigation, and skill-building — never harm.

## Evidence handling

- Hash every evidence file (SHA-256) and record the value.
- Work only on copies; original evidence stays read-only.
- Keep an unbroken chain of custody — who, what, when, why.
- Verify integrity again when analysis ends.

## Conduct

- Do not attempt unauthorised access to any system.
- Do not publish anything that could expose real people or organisations.
- Raise any legal or ethical concern with the Legal & Digital Evidence Advisor before proceeding.

## A shared standard

Every selected member agrees to these rules before they start. If a case cannot be run legally and ethically, we do not run it.
