## What we are

FORENSYRA is a **Digital Evidence & DFIR Collective** for students and early-career professionals. We share knowledge, build projects together, and grow into a strong team in digital forensics, incident response, and evidence-led investigation.

Our motto is simple: **Preserve the Trace. Prove the Story.**

## Why we exist

Digital forensics is growing quickly, but many learners study it alone — mostly theory, with no real case practice. We bring people together so they can:

- learn from each other and from mentors,
- practise on legal, authorised mock cases, and
- build real, public work as a team.

For every member, the result is a stronger portfolio, better internship and job chances, and a place on a team that stands out.

## How we work

- **Roles first.** Every member owns one of thirteen defined specialisations, filled in phases.
- **One strong project.** We aim to finish one complete investigation rather than many partial ones.
- **Light and sustainable.** One short call plus about an hour of task work a week.
- **Open by default.** Our work lives in a public GitHub organisation.

## Leadership

Forensyra is founded and led by **Gopi Chand**, our Lead Forensic Investigator, who sets the direction of each case and approves the published reports.
