A growing, curated starting point. We keep this list practical and legal.

## Public sample evidence

- **Digital Corpora** — public disk images and filesystem corpora for practice.
- **Volatility memory samples** — public RAM dumps for memory-forensics exercises.
- **Wireshark sample captures** — public PCAPs for network-traffic analysis.
- **NIST CFReDS** — reference data sets for digital-forensics tool testing.

## Tools we use

- **Disk & endpoint:** Autopsy, The Sleuth Kit, FTK Imager (free), Eric Zimmerman tools
- **Memory & malware:** Volatility 3, strings, PE analysis tooling
- **Network:** Wireshark, tshark, NetworkMiner
- **Timeline & reporting:** Plaso / log2timeline, Timeline Explorer
- **Mobile:** free and open-source extraction and parsing tools

## Learn

- **DFIR learning:** public CTF and challenge archives focused on forensics
- **Standards:** NIST SP 800-86 (integrating forensics into incident response)
- **Documentation:** our own case reports and write-ups in this site

> Only use tools and evidence you are authorised to use, and always follow our [ethics rules](/ethics).
