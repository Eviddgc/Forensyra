Before any analysis begins, the Evidence Custodian records what we have and proves it has not changed.

## Our basic rules

1. **Hash everything.** Every evidence file is hashed (SHA-256) and the value is recorded.
2. **Work on copies.** Original evidence is read-only; analysis happens on working copies.
3. **Log every action.** Who accessed what, when, and why — the chain of custody stays unbroken.
4. **Verify at the end.** Re-hash to confirm nothing was altered during analysis.

These habits are the difference between an interesting story and a defensible finding.
