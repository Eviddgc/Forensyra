Digital forensics is growing fast, but many students learn it alone — mostly theory, with no real case practice. Forensyra exists to change that.

We are a collective of students and early-career professionals who learn together, practise on legal and authorised mock cases, and build a strong, evidence-led portfolio as a team.

## What we value

- **Preserve the trace.** Evidence integrity comes first — hash it, track it, protect it.
- **Prove the story.** Analysis only matters when it is validated and explained clearly.
- **Learn in the open.** Our work is public and reusable.
- **Stay legal and ethical.** Practice material only, never real victim data.

If that sounds like you, [apply to join](/join).
