# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

FORENSYRA exists to make digital forensics more open and more trustworthy. We
want anyone to be able to learn here — regardless of experience, background,
identity, nationality, disability, appearance, religion or skill level.

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds this community:

- Being kind and patient with people who are learning.
- Giving and accepting constructive, specific feedback.
- Assuming good faith, and asking questions instead of accusing.
- Respecting that evidence, cases and personal data are sensitive.
- Owning mistakes, apologising, and moving on.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal and political attacks.
- Sexualised language or attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without explicit permission.
- Posting real case material, stolen evidence, or data you are not authorised
  to share — this is both harmful and possibly illegal.
- Encouraging anyone to break the law, or to use these skills against people
  without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or dismissing others' contributions.

## Ethics of the work

Everything shared in this community must follow the [ethics](/ethics) we hold
ourselves to: lawful, authorised, consented, and evidence-preserving. Anyone
who asks for help breaking into systems, hiding traces, or handling material
they have no right to hold will be removed.

## Scope

This code applies in all community spaces — the repository, issues and pull
requests, and any chat or call where you represent FORENSYRA.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **forensyra@outlook.in**. Reports are handled
privately.

Responses, in order of severity:

1. **Correction** — a private note explaining the problem, and a request to
   change the behaviour.
2. **Warning** — a formal warning with consequences for repeating it.
3. **Temporary ban** — a defined period away from the community.
4. **Permanent ban** — removal from all spaces, for serious or repeated harm.

Those who enforce this code will act fairly, and will not disclose the identity
of the reporter.
