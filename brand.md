# Forensyra brand guide

Keep this short and consistent. It is the reference for how Forensyra looks and
sounds across the website, GitHub, and social posts.

## Identity

| Element | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Name | **FORENSYRA** (always uppercase, one word) |
| Descriptor | Digital Evidence & DFIR Collective |
| Tagline | Where Digital Truth Takes Form. |
| Motto | Preserve the Trace. Prove the Story. |

## Logo

- Files: `Photos/Logo.png` (master), `public/logo.png` (web, 512px),
  `src/app/icon.png` (favicon), `src/app/apple-icon.png`.
- The mark is circular. Give it a little breathing room; do not stretch it,
  recolour it, or place it on a busy background.
- The site already uses it in the header, footer, and as the social/OG image.

## Colour

Dark "forensic lab" palette. Tokens live in `src/app/globals.css`.

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `background` | `#05070d` | Page background |
| `surface` | `#0a0f1a` | Cards and panels |
| `surface-2` | `#0f172a` | Raised surfaces |
| `border` | `#1e293b` | Dividers and outlines |
| `foreground` | `#e2e8f0` | Primary text |
| `muted` | `#94a3b8` | Secondary text |
| `accent` | `#22d3ee` | Brand accent (cyan) |
| `accent-2` | `#818cf8` | Secondary accent (indigo) |
| `success` | `#34d399` | Positive states |
| `warning` | `#fbbf24` | Pinned / attention |

## Type

- **Sans:** Geist — body and headings.
- **Mono:** Geist Mono — labels, hex/binary accents, uppercase eyebrows.

## Voice

- Precise and calm. Evidence-led, never sensational.
- Prefer "shows", "indicates", "preserved" over "proves" (except our motto).
- Always legal and ethical: practice cases and public sample evidence only.
